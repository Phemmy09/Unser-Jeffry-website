5. VSL — Video Sales Letter
A scripted, high-production video embedded at the top of the landing page. This is the primary sales mechanism.
5.1 Content Brief
Hook: Pattern interrupt — something visually compelling in the first 3 seconds
Problem: Pain points of freelancers / aspiring agency owners who can't scale
Agitate: Show the gap between where they are and where they want to be
Solution: Introduce the course as the roadmap
Pitch: Walk through the offer stack (Discord, Whop, calls, events, DM access)
Social proof: Results, testimonials, credibility indicators
CTA: Direct application prompt with urgency framing
5.2 Production Notes
Script first — then record with confidence
Cool cinematic edits, B-roll, text overlays — high production value
Flexing context (lifestyle, proof) woven in naturally — not cringe, aspirational
Edit handled by Ali / Bilal Faseeh or Jon (videographer)

6.1 Two Segments

10. ManyChat Premium — Lead Generation Automation
ManyChat automations are configured to trigger off comments and DMs on TikTok and Instagram content, enabling passive lead capture at scale.
10.1 Trigger Flows
CTA videos about AI tools or walkthroughs → comment or DM a keyword to receive a freebie
Triggered DM sends a link to a freebie opt-in page
10.2 Freebie Opt-In Flow
Viewer comments a keyword or DMs (triggered by ManyChat)
ManyChat sends a link to the freebie opt-in landing page
User submits email on opt-in page
Freebie delivered via email automatically (Kit)
Email enters unpaid nurture sequence (re-engagement → course CTA)
